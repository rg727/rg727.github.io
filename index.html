About

As a postdoc, I work on better resolving historical estimates of Great Lake lake levels to inform forecasts under our changing climate. My PhD was focused on developing tree-ring based reconstructions of weather regime dynamics to create hydroclimate scenarios that more fully capture natural hydroclimate variability in California over the past 600 years. I explored how hydroclimate extremes propagate through California’s complex water system down to individual users. Read more about my work below!
